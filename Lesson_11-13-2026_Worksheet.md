## Part 1: The 4-Step Game Pitch Blueprint
Use this structure to teach 1–2 favorite board games (e.g., *7 Wonders*, *Lupus in Tabula*):
1. **The Hook (1 sentence):** What's the theme and vibe?
2. **The Goal:** How do you win?
3. **Turn Sequence:** *"On your turn, you can either A, B, or C..."*
4. **The Strategy Twist:** What separates a beginner from a winner?
