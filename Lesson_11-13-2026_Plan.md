## 🎯 Teaching Philosophy for This Lesson
Simone is warm, analytical, and a board-game enthusiast from Bologna (*7 Wonders*, *Risk*, *Lupus in Tabula*). Explaining game rules in a second language is one of the **best real-world clarity workouts** because it forces **sequencing** (*first, on your turn, either... or...*), **conditionals** (*if you draw X, then...*), and **concise summaries**.
- **Correction Rule:** During his game pitch, act like an engaged first-time player—ask genuine clarifying questions when something is ambiguous instead of correcting grammar mid-flow. Save `-ed` and phrasing polishes for the debrief!

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Script

### 1. Warm-Up: Catching Up from Bologna (00:00 – 08:00)
- **What to ask:** *"How was your week in Bologna? How are the twins—any chaotic or funny moments? Played any good games or watched any films lately?"*
- **Listening check:** Gently notice `-ed` endings (*played, started, wanted*) and encourage natural English connectors (`actually`, `basically`, `the thing is`, `in other words`) instead of literal Italian transitions.

### 2. Core Activity: Teach Louie Two Board Games (08:00 – 30:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **Setup (2 mins):** *"Last time we talked about teaching me 1–2 of your favorite board games. Today I'm a total beginner sitting at your table in Bologna! Use the 4-step blueprint on the canvas so I don't get lost."*
  1. **The Hook (1 sentence):** Theme & vibe.
  2. **The Goal:** How do you win?
  3. **Turn Sequence:** *"On your turn, you can either A, B, or C..."*
  4. **The Strategy Twist:** What separates a beginner from a winner?
- **Game 1 Pitch (~10 mins):** Have Simone explain Game #1 (e.g., *7 Wonders* or *Lupus in Tabula*).
  - **Your Role (Playful Beginner):** Interrupt politely 2–3 times with realistic player questions:
    - *"Wait—so if I run out of coins, what happens on my turn?"*
    - *"Hold on, can I do both A and B, or just one?"*
    - *"So how do you actually win—most points, or last player standing?"*
- **Quick Structure Tune-Up (2 mins):** Highlight 1 great explanation move he used + 1 connector upgrade (*"Try 'In other words...' when a rule sounds complicated"*).
- **Game 2 Pitch (~8 mins):** Repeat with a second, different style of game (e.g., social deduction vs. resource drafting), aiming for an even crisper **Hook + Turn Sequence**.

### 3. Strategy & Persuasion Idioms (30:00 – 43:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **Worksheet Part 2 Answer Key:** `1. bluff your way through` • `2. had an ace up her sleeve` • `3. level the playing field` • `4. turned the tables` • `5. read him like a book` • `6. a calculated risk`.
- **How to teach it:**
  1. Complete the 6 blanks together (5 mins), checking past tense (`had`, `turned`) and articles (`a calculated risk`).
  2. **Bridge to Real Life (6 mins):** Ask Simone 3 quick questions tying the idioms to **both** board games and work/life:
     - *"In Lupus in Tabula—or in a meeting—are you good at **bluffing your way through**, or can people **read you like a book**?"*
     - *"Tell me about a time you took **a calculated risk**."*
     - *"What's one **ace up your sleeve** in your favorite game?"*

### 4. Wrap-Up & Homework (43:00 – 50:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- Share **3 strong expressions** Simone used + **2 gentle phrasing/`-ed` polishes**.
- **Homework:** Pick **3 idioms** from today and write 1 short sentence for each (either a board-game example or a workplace example).

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue & Extension
- **If Game 1 takes the whole 22 minutes:** Skip Game 2 and go straight to Step 3—ask him to apply all 6 idioms directly to scenarios inside Game 1!
- **If he finishes early:** Rapid-fire *"Pitch a famous movie or classic game (like Chess or Monopoly) in exactly 3 sentences using the Hook → Goal → Twist formula."*
