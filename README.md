# 🍂 Simone's Peaceful English Sanctuary & Lesson 01 Companion

A calm, interactive digital sanctuary and lesson notes companion created for **Simone's first English conversation lesson with Louie** (October 21, 2026).

---

## 🌟 What's Inside Simone's Lesson 01

1. **📜 Full Cleaned Conversation Transcript (`00:00` – `50:50`)**:
   - Every speaking turn from the 50-minute lesson organized across 6 thematic chapters:
     - **Chapter I**: Welcome & The Workplace English Foundation (`00:00` – `05:30`)
     - **Chapter II**: Workplace Vocabulary & The System Demo (`05:34` – `15:00`)
     - **Chapter III**: Quick Word Association & The Italian Table (`15:10` – `20:30`)
     - **Chapter IV**: Life in Bologna, Twins & Family Ties (`20:38` – `26:10`)
     - **Chapter V**: Board Games, Gaming & A New Zealand Honeymoon (`26:14` – `41:35`)
     - **Chapter VI**: Cultural Contrasts, Lord of the Rings & Looking Ahead (`41:40` – `50:50`)
   - **52 inline highlighted vocabulary words, phrasal verbs, and idioms** (`B2`–`C2`) right where they were spoken, with hover tooltips and a live sticky **Vocabulary Spotlight** side panel.
   - All automated speech-to-text / note-taker mishearings forensically fixed inline with toggleable pills (`fixBadge`).

2. **📚 Timestamped Vocabulary & Idioms Dictionary**:
   - Searchable and filterable grid of all 52 flagged words by level (`B2`, `C1`, `C2`) and search query.
   - Includes British English (`en-GB`) audio pronunciation, phonetic IPA, exact timestamps with instant jump-to-turn links (`🕒 MM:SS ↗`), comprehensive definitions, exact lesson quotes, and Italian equivalents (*Connessione in italiano*).

3. **✨ Note-Taker Fixes & Gentle Phrasing Notes**:
   - **Your Natural Strengths**: Celebrating 6 natural expressions and intuitive concepts Simone used brilliantly (building confidence first!).
   - **Gentle Phrasing Polishes**: 6 supportive, non-intimidating phrasing upgrades (*"In the moment"* vs *"Natural Polish"*) with audio and grammatical explanations.
   - **Note-Taker Clean-Up Log**: Visual log of 13 automated note-taker spelling errors corrected (*"Tom Bombay"* → *"Tom Bombadil"*, *"Lupo Lupis"* → *"Lupus in Tabula"*, *"Hobbiton Town"* → *"Hobbiton"*, *"boarding games"* → *"board games"*, *"three capital"* → *"three couples"*, *"civil team"* → *"civilization"*, *"Vinegard"* → *"vineyard / vines"*, etc.).

4. **🏛️ Cultural Places & Topics Explored**:
   - 4 rich editorial cards:
     1. *Bologna & The Porticoes of Emilia-Romagna*
     2. *Hanoi & Vietnam: Life on Two Wheels*
     3. *Hobbiton & The Landscapes of New Zealand*
     4. *The Modern Tabletop Renaissance (7 Wonders, Risk & Lupus in Tabula)*

5. **☕ Peaceful Practice Sanctuary**:
   - Interactive 12-card flashcard flip deck (`FLASHCARD_KEYS`).
   - 6-question no-pressure context matching quiz with celebratory confetti.
   - 3 thoughtful reflection prompts to prepare for the next lesson (introducing two board games to Louie!).

6. **🌬️ Calming Atmosphere & Sensory Plugins**:
   - **Procedural Web Audio API Soundscape**: Choose between *Kensington Autumn Breeze*, *Soft London Rain*, and *Warm Study Hearth* (never relies on external MP3s).
   - **Interactive HTML5 Canvas Autumn Breeze**: Falling autumn leaf animation with toggleable speed (*Gentle*, *Breeze*, *Still*).
   - **3 Restful Color Moods**: *Bath Stone (Default)*, *Winchester Mist*, and *Warm Study*.
   - **Speech Synthesis Audio**: Adjustable UK voice speed (`0.85×` and `1.0×`) for all vocabulary, phrases, and turns.

## 🚀 GitHub Pages Deployment
This repository is configured for zero-build static hosting via GitHub Pages:
- `index.html`: Self-contained single-page application using Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, GSAP, Tippy.js, and Canvas Confetti via CDN.
- `.nojekyll`: Bypasses Jekyll processing so static assets load cleanly.
